# Walking Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy covers the Walking Buddy Android app (com.walkingbuddy.app), maintained through the ChiIIerr/Walking-Buddy project. It also applies to the separately installed debug build.

## Information used on your device

Walking Buddy reads your phone's step counter only after you enable automatic tracking and grant Physical activity permission where Android requires it. It stores step totals, dates, daily goals, walking history, streaks, pet choices and nicknames, care levels, rewards, preferences, and counter baselines locally. These records provide your progress, journal, and virtual-pet features. A device reboot counter is used locally to recognize when the step sensor resets; it is not an advertising or tracking identifier.

Automatic tracking continues when the app is in the background. A health foreground service and an ongoing notification keep the sensor registered. You can pause tracking in Settings or from the notification, or revoke Physical activity permission in Android settings. If you previously enabled tracking, it may resume after a phone restart or app update. Manual entry remains available without sensor permission. Notification permission controls notification visibility; denying it does not itself turn off step tracking.

The app does not access GPS location, contacts, heart rate, Health Connect, or watch accounts. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, third-party runtime SDKs, or Internet permission. We do not receive, collect on our servers, sell, or automatically share your walking or pet data.

## Sharing, files, and external links

When you choose Share my progress, Android sends the displayed steps, buddy nickname, streak, and lifetime total to the app or recipient you select. Export and backup save a CSV or JSON file to a location you choose through Android's file picker. A selected cloud storage provider may upload that file under its own policies. Restoring a backup reads only the file you select. These actions happen at your request.

Exports are readable, unencrypted files. Keep them somewhere you trust. Other apps and recipients handle files and messages under their own policies. Opening the online policy or contacting support opens an external browser or app; that service may receive ordinary connection information and anything you submit. Do not post private walking history or backups in public GitHub issues.

## Retention and deletion

Progress remains in the app's private storage until you reset it, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall it. Settings > Reset all progress deletes local app records and stops tracking. Automatic Android backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. The app may keep a local recovery copy when a backup is restored or damaged; reset/clear/uninstall removes those copies too.

Files you export and information you send to other apps remain outside Walking Buddy. Delete those separately from your chosen storage providers or recipients. There is no server account to delete and we cannot recover deleted local progress.

## Purpose and changes

Walking Buddy is a general wellness and virtual-pet app. Step counts and distance estimates may be incomplete or inaccurate. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Seek a qualified healthcare professional's advice for medical questions. Your buddy's health is a game mechanic, not an assessment of your health.

If the app's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. Any newly required permissions will be requested in the app.
