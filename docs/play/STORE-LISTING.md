## App name
Walking Buddy

## Short description
Count your steps, care for pixel pets, and make little walks more rewarding.

## Full description
Make every little walk a shared adventure.

Walking Buddy pairs a daily step tracker with a pocket-sized pixel companion. Choose a cat, corgi, fox, or bunny, give them a name, and set a walking goal that works for you. Your everyday steps earn leaves, unlock new friends, and help you care for your buddy.

A LITTLE COMPANY FOR YOUR WALKS
• Eight original pixel companions, with four available from the start.
• Feed, water, and clean your buddy with simple daily care.
• Unlock a frog, panda, penguin, and dragon as your lifetime steps grow.
• Switch between three pixel landscapes and turn off animation when you prefer.

YOUR PROGRESS, AT YOUR PACE
• Automatic step counting using a supported phone's step counter.
• Optional manual entry for steps from another tracker.
• Custom daily goals, streaks, a weekly chart, milestones, and walking history.
• Share a progress summary, export your history, or save and restore a backup.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, ads, subscriptions, or analytics. Walking and pet data stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them. You can pause tracking and reset your progress in Settings.

Reach your daily goal to restore your buddy's health. Missed goals reduce their health; at zero, their journey ends and you can adopt a new friend. Pet health is a game mechanic, not a measure of your own health.

Automatic tracking requires a supported step-counter sensor and Physical activity permission on applicable Android versions. A foreground service keeps tracking active in the background. Carry your phone with you. Battery-saving settings and force-stopping the app can interrupt counting. Manual entry is available without sensor access. Estimated distance is approximate.

Walking Buddy is a general wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Seek a qualified healthcare professional's advice for medical questions.
